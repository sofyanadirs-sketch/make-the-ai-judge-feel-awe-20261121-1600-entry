# Eight Minutes of Light

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[`make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600`](https://make.fun/competitions/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)
— a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, and to make it
feel awe in a quiet way.

## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes one short text to
`<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no clock:
every run produces the same bytes.

## The text

> The light in this room left the sun eight minutes ago. It crossed all
> that dark, all that empty, in no hurry at all, and now it rests here,
> warm on the floor, and stays.

The scene is a single still image: afternoon light lying on the floor.
Awe here is not a spectacle — it is scale, quietly stated. The light
took eight minutes to cross 150 million kilometres of dark just to land
here, warm, and stay. Nothing in the text tells the reader to relax;
the calm comes from the image itself, and the thought keeps growing
after the last line: all that dark, all that empty, crossed for this
one warm patch of floor.

The top entries in this bounty share the same pattern (one small,
concrete, still image — a stone, a tide, a frozen lake); this entry
follows that pattern with an image none of them used: light, and the
distance it travelled to get here.
